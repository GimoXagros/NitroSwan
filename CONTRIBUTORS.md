## Identity and contribution counts

GitHub's Contributors panel is generated by GitHub, not a manually maintained
release roster. It may lag or differ from `git shortlog -sne main` because of
account/email association, merge counting and caching.

The repository `.mailmap` normalizes the existing GimoXagros/Xagros author
aliases to the account's verified public noreply identity and normalizes the
Unicode spelling of Fredrik Ahlström. It does not rewrite commits, fabricate
contributions or guarantee an immediate change to GitHub's panel.
Original authorship and release SHAs are preserved.
